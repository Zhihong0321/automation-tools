Petrol stations are back in as leads (26 Oct 2026). The team has petrol station clients, so the Petrol Station and Gas station categories and the station brands (Petronas, Shell, Petron, Caltex, BHPetrol) were removed from the blacklist. 334 station leads are now open for contact research. Chain shops at a station still count as their chain (Subway @ Caltex, FamilyMart Petron).

How a lead is judged

  • Landmarks are stripped first: “beside Burger King” is not Burger King.
  • Out if its Google category is on a group’s list, or its name has a group’s word.
  • “Solar” alone is not a competitor (tint, watch and LPG shops stay in).

What it stops

  • The contact research auto-queue.
  • A taman’s “Research N pending” count and button.
  • Not stopped: research started by hand, the lead list, telemarketing.

Worth knowing

  • 715 blacklisted leads were researched before the blacklist existed. Their reports are kept.
  • Office and shop-lot tenants (consultants, salons, agencies) are left in on purpose.
  • Every lead is re-checked on each deploy.
