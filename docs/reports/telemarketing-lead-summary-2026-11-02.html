How it was counted

Definitions

Unique company

One per company. The 176,375 live Google Maps listings fold to 163,620: branches of one registered name (Sdn Bhd, Bhd, PLT) are already merged, and 12,755 more branch listings that share one website fold into 3,205 chains.

Website

The company's own site. Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Shopee, link-in-bio and similar shared pages do not count.

Email address

Any email read off the company website or found by contact research and company deep research.

Mobile phone

A number in Malaysian mobile format, 01xxxxxxxx(x) or +601xxxxxxxx(x), from Google Maps, the website or research. Office lines are left out.

Mobile with a person name

A mobile number research ties to a named person (decision maker or validated contact), not a company line.

Person name

Every named person research found, counted once per company. Unnamed company lines are not people.

Counts every live lead in the call list, including 23,517 blacklisted and 6,622 do-not-call listings. Hidden and merged listings are left out. Read from the lead database on 2 Nov 2026.
