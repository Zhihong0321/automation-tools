What we learned

Cold email is legal. Inboxes punish mass mailing.

The law
  • Malaysia has no law banning B2B cold email
  • PDPA applies: be honest, offer an opt-out
  • Singapore and the EU are stricter
The inbox providers
  • Google and Microsoft filter anything that looks like mass mailing
  • They watch volume, bounces and spam reports
  • Penalty: spam folder, blocked mailbox, damaged domain

The real risk is not a fine. It is losing the ability to email anyone, including our customers.

The difficulty now

Dead and wrong email addresses

Why addresses bounce
  • Staff leave; their personal company address is deleted
  • Old websites list addresses nobody reads
  • Typos on directories and Maps listings
Why it matters
  • Safe bounce rate: under 3%. Ours today: 20% (1 of 5)
  • Providers read high bounces as a sign of spam sending
  • A new inbox with high bounces lands in spam for good

We cannot check an address from our office: our internet provider blocks the mail port used for checking.

The plan for bounces

Free fixes first, a paid check only if needed

01

Company addresses first

info@, sales@, enquiry@ before a named person's address; shared addresses rarely disappear

02

Fast brake

Stop adding leads after 2 bounces in a row, or above 5% after 10 sent

03

Never twice

A bounced address, and its company, is never emailed again

04

Paid check, if bounces stay high

An address checking service, about US$37–39 per 10,000 checks, before each send

Steps 1 to 3 cost nothing. Step 4 is decided after two to three weeks of real bounce numbers.
